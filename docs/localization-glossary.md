# Localization Glossary

The terms a Quran-reading app repeats on every screen, fixed once so they do not drift between packs, and so a reviewer has one page to check rather than five files.

> **These translations have not been reviewed by a speaker of any of these languages.** They were drafted with care and read by the team (`translationStatus: "ai-drafted"`). Nothing here is a religious ruling, and a qualified teacher should confirm every term in the tajwid section before this is presented as teaching material.

## Policy

1. **Keep the Arabic term where learners already use it.** Every one of these languages is spoken by communities who learn the Quran with the Arabic vocabulary — *madd*, *sukoon*, *shaddah*, *tajweed*, *makhraj*. Translating them into an invented native equivalent would be less clear, not more.
2. **Explain on first use, then use the term.** A lesson that introduces *tanween* says what it is in the learner's own words, and afterwards calls it tanween.
3. **Never transliterate into Latin script** inside a non-Latin pack. Write the Arabic term in the pack's own script.
4. **Keep one form per term per language.** The right-hand columns below are the only accepted spellings.
5. **Interface words are translated, teaching terms are kept.** "Listen", "Repeat", "Review" are ordinary words and belong in the learner's language; *qalqalah* is a name and stays.

Where a pack has two forms above (فتحه / زور), the first is the Arabic term and the second the everyday local name. Lessons introduce both once and then use the Arabic term.

### Recitation terms — teacher review required

| English | Pashto | Dari | Urdu | Arabic |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tajweed | تجوید | تجوید | تجوید | التجويد |
| makhraj (articulation point) | مخرج | مخرج | مخرج | المخرج |
| qalqalah | قلقله | قلقله | قلقلہ | القلقلة |
| ghunnah | غنه | غنه | غنہ | الغنة |
| noon sakinah | نون ساکنه | نون ساکنه | نون ساکنہ | النون الساكنة |
| meem sakinah | میم ساکنه | میم ساکنه | میم ساکنہ | الميم الساكنة |
| waqf (stopping) | وقف | وقف | وقف | الوقف |

**None of these appear as a claim about the learner's recitation.** The app can say a qalqalah letter is on the page; it cannot say a qalqalah was performed. That boundary is enforced in the curriculum tests and stated in every lesson that names one of these terms.
